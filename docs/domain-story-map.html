What changed, and why

  • The Friend now sees the trip. Sentence 6 is new. The old story invited a friend who then vanished for eleven sentences — the scope's own destination was never drawn.
  • Trip Service became an actor. Nobody delivered the invitation, and the Highlight Reel appeared from nowhere. Sentences 4 and 10 give both an origin.
  • Three looks at verbs are gone. Replaced with relives and retraces — what a person says when there is no phone in their hand.
  • creates → starts, shares → adds … to. One is CRUD, the other was ambiguous about who could see it.
  • Actor labels collapsed from six to three, then aligned to the glossary. Organizer, Participant, Organizer and User were four names for two people and one system. The glossary is the authority: Organizer and Friend.
  • The Map is gone. It had no entry in the visual glossary and no bearing on the friend seeing the trip. If map-based browsing matters, it belongs to a story of its own.

Parked — Story 2: Public Discovery

Six sentences with a different actor, a different motivation, and no causal link to the story above. Not wasted, just not this story. It is also where the consent problem lives.

Settle these before you freeze

Three open questions

1 · Does anyone really invite before there is anything to see? Sentence 3 assumes the collaboration is set up first. Ask everyone at the table what they actually did after their last shared trip. If nobody set an album up in advance, sentences 1–3 are aspirational and the order should invert.

2 · When is a Trip finished? No sentence closes it. Your metric counts trips, so something has to define when one is done.

3 · Is Moment one thing or two? A photo dropped in and a highlighted keepsake behave differently in sentences 2, 9 and 10. Station 4 will force the answer.

Left uncut on purpose

No lanes, no groups, no colour-coded regions. The language visibly changes somewhere in this map — that seam is yours to find at 19:32, and pre-drawing it would hand you the answer to the exercise. Draw your rings on paper first, then compare.
